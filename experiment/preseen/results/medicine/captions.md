# Figure captions — Physiology or Medicine

## option_probabilities.png

Forecast probability of each option (rows, in question order; labels shortened, full texts in questions/medicine.json). control: blue circles (n = 4 runs); treat: orange triangles (n = 3 runs). Small hollow markers = individual runs; large filled marker = mean over runs. Arms are offset vertically within a row.

## treat_minus_control.png

Difference between the mean treat probability and the mean control probability per option (orange triangle). Grey bar = the control arm's run-to-run range (min to max over the 4 control runs), centred on the control mean (0); a difference outside the grey bar is larger than the spread between control runs.
