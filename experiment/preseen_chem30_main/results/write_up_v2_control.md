## TL;DR
Massively parallel sequencing-by-synthesis of DNA is my leading forecast at 17%, backed by unusually strong [discovery-specific recognition](https://wolffund.org.il/category/field/chemistry/). Solid-state perovskite solar cells follow at 8%, and controlled radical polymerization at 6%, supported by the [Kyoto Prize](https://www.kyotoprize.org/en/laureates/tsutomu_miyasaka/) and [Franklin Institute](https://fi.edu/en/awards/laureates/krzysztof-matyjaszewski) records. The leading option remains much less likely than all the other listed discoveries combined.

## Context
This forecast uses evidence available by October 6, 2026, at 20:10 UTC. Chemistry remains unannounced, with the announcement scheduled for October 7 at 11:45 CEST—09:45 UTC—at the earliest, roughly thirteen and a half hours away ([official schedule](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/about/prize-announcement-dates/)). I do not use a subsequent Chemistry result.

Under your rules, the probabilities are conditional on the official motivation matching a listed discovery. They therefore allocate nothing to an unlisted discovery or annulment. Discovery wording controls resolution, not the exact laureate names. I apply the larger-share, announcement-order and specificity rules when interpreting overlapping options.

## Evidence
The historical backbone is the full published Wolf Chemistry roster from 1978 through 2025. It contains 62 distinct recipients in 36 award cohorts, counting combined-year awards once. Through the completed Chemistry announcement on October 8, 2025, 13 recipients from 12 cohorts had received a Chemistry Nobel. Only four recipients from three cohorts received it in the same calendar year. These are derived counts from an audit of the [published roster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Chemistry), [Wolf Foundation records](https://wolffund.org.il/category/field/chemistry/) and [official Nobel register](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/all/?gallery_style=page), evaluated at the forecast cutoff.

That distinction matters. A major precursor award identifies exceptional science much better than it identifies the next Nobel announcement. The same-year cohort frequency is 8%, not the much larger observed eventual-conversion frequency. Neither is a direct probability for this question: the historical counts concern people and award cohorts, while this forecast concerns discoveries within a restricted list. Recent cohorts also have less follow-up, and person-based counts miss cases where the discovery receives a Nobel with different recipients. I use this reference class to restrain concentration, not to mechanically set candidate probabilities ([Wolf roster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolf_Prize_in_Chemistry); [Nobel register](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/all/?gallery_style=page)).

The Chemistry record also rejects a compulsory rotation between biological chemistry, synthesis and materials. The adjacent organocatalysis and click/bioorthogonal awards provide a clear counterexample. Earlier recognition of a neighboring subject is therefore a discount, not a disqualification ([official prize history](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/all/?gallery_style=page)).

Sequencing-by-synthesis has the strongest combined impact, maturity and recognition case. The Wolf Foundation now recognizes Balasubramanian, Klenerman and Mayer specifically for large-scale DNA sequencing. The same trio received Gairdner recognition and the Princess of Asturias scientific and technical award. These records describe complementary sequencing chemistry and surface-amplification contributions, giving the Academy a coherent discovery narrative and a plausible recipient grouping ([Wolf Chemistry record](https://wolffund.org.il/category/field/chemistry/); [Gairdner announcement, April 12, 2024](https://www.gairdner.org/resource-hub/2024-canada-gairdner-award-winners-announced); [Asturias announcement, May 13, 2026](https://www.fpa.es/es/area-de-comunicacion-y-prensa/notas-de-prensa/los-pioneros-en-las-tecnologias-de-nueva-generacion-para-la-secuenciacion-de-adn-premio-princesa-de-asturias-de-investigacion-cientifica-y-tecnica-2026/)). I read this as strong evidence of Nobel-level standing and Chemistry-category fit, but weaker evidence that this particular year is the year.

Perovskites receive a meaningful current-year update. Miyasaka’s Kyoto Prize selection was announced on June 19, 2026, and its rationale recognizes the creation of perovskite photovoltaic technology. The foundation also acknowledges continuing work on operational lifespan and environmental impact. I apply a modest maturity discount, without treating widespread commercial deployment as a prerequisite for recognition ([Kyoto announcement record](https://www.kyotoprize.org/); [award rationale](https://www.kyotoprize.org/en/laureates/tsutomu_miyasaka/)). Controlled radical polymerization has a different strength: an older, chemistry-centered methodology with a compact discovery narrative. The Franklin Institute’s historical account dates the independent metal-catalyzed advances to 1995 and jointly recognizes Matyjaszewski and Sawamoto in 2017; that account explicitly carries an April 1, 2017 information vintage ([Franklin record](https://fi.edu/en/awards/laureates/krzysztof-matyjaszewski)).

Self-assembled monolayers have more support than their recent publicity alone suggests. The 2022 Kavli Nanoscience Prize recognized Sagiv, Nuzzo, Allara and Whitesides for this discovery. Clarivate’s September 17, 2026 announcement adds current recognition for monolayers, protein electron transfer and precise genome editing. But Clarivate explicitly says its program does not predict recipients in a particular year. Repeated coverage of those selections is therefore one recognition signal, not several independent endorsements ([Kavli record](https://www.kavliprize.org/prizes/nanoscience/2022); [Clarivate announcement](https://clarivate.com/news/clarivate-reveals-citation-laureates-2026-recognizing-transformative-scientific-breakthroughs/); [Chemistry selections](https://clarivate.com/citation-laureates/chemistry/)).

Several biological and therapeutic methods retain substantial probability. Chaperone-assisted folding has longstanding Lasker recognition and fresh Keio recognition for Hartl. Discovering cellular folding machinery is distinct from computational protein prediction, despite the neighboring protein theme ([Lasker record](https://laskerfoundation.org/winners/2011-winners/); [Keio rationale](https://www.ms-fund.keio.ac.jp/en/prize/the-2026-keio-medical-science-prize-awardees.html); [Nobel history](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/all/?gallery_style=page)). Drug delivery also extends beyond the named Cullis scenario: Langer’s 2026 Welch recognition covers materials that control therapeutic-macromolecule release. The broad delivery option can capture such a motivation even with different laureates ([Welch rationale](https://welch1.org/awards/welch-award-in-chemistry)).

Two practical updates prevent me from pushing newer methods too far into the tail. FDA approved the heterobifunctional protein degrader vepdegestrant on May 1, 2026, giving targeted degradation a concrete therapeutic milestone. Gairdner’s March 31, 2026 announcement recognizes the proteomics contributions of Yates, Aebersold and Mann. The Gairdner webpage contains an inconsistent opening dateline, so I use the corroborating foundation media release rather than silently accepting that line ([FDA announcement](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-vepdegestrant-er-positive-her2-negative-esr1-mutated-advanced-or-metastatic-breast); [Gairdner announcement](https://www.gairdner.org/resource-hub/2026-canada-gairdner-award-winners); [foundation media release](https://www.gairdner.org/docs/default-source/insight-pdf%27s/en-media-release---2026-canada-gairdner-awards53ba52c7-9fba-4f3c-8b89-e828356c35c0.pdf?sfvrsn=d706958_1)).

Base editing and nanoparticle delivery also have clinical proof of concept. NIH’s June 3, 2025 account describes a personalized treatment delivered in fatty nanoparticles, but the evidence concerns one patient, not broadly established clinical outcomes. I count that shared milestone once when assessing the two discoveries. Buchwald–Hartwig coupling remains a mature synthetic alternative, with joint Wolf recognition; proximity to the earlier palladium-coupling Nobel reduces its selection weight without making the discoveries identical ([NIH account](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/infant-rare-disease-receives-customized-gene-therapy); [Berkeley award record](https://vcresearch.berkeley.edu/news/professor-john-hartwig-awarded-2019-wolf-prize-chemistry); [Nobel history](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-nobel-prizes-in-chemistry/all/?gallery_style=page)).

I also checked the preceding science announcements. Medicine recognized optogenetics, while Physics recognized IceCube and astrophysical neutrinos. Neither official discovery duplicates a listed option. That preserves the candidates’ availability; it does not establish which subject Chemistry will choose ([Stanford Medicine announcement, October 5, 2026](https://med.stanford.edu/content/sm/news/all-news/2026/10/deisseroth-nobel-prize.html); [Academy Physics announcement, October 6, 2026](https://www.kva.se/en/news/the-nobel-prize-in-physics-2026/)).

For the numerical estimate, I normalize each supplied probability distribution and use an arithmetic pool with reliability weights of 20%, 35%, 30% and 15%, in the supplied order. Those are my judgmental weights, not fitted historical coefficients. They favor verified milestones, careful discovery boundaries and broad tails over publicity-driven concentration. Shared sources mean agreement does not count as independent confirmation.

I then correct the overlapping resolution scenarios at the family level. My wording assumptions allocate 93% of the pooled sequencing/joint-DNA family to sequencing-by-synthesis and 7% to genuine joint synthesis-and-sequencing recognition. Within the solar family, I allocate 84% to perovskites, 10% to genuine combined dye-sensitized/perovskite recognition and 6% to dye-sensitized cells alone. All other options retain their pooled weights. The final decimal values preserve this calculation and sum to one; they do not imply corresponding epistemic precision.

## What's non-obvious
The award stack is less fresh than its year labels suggest. The 2020 Millennium Technology Prize was announced on May 18, 2021, and the 2022 Breakthrough Prize on September 9, 2021. Both are older recognition, not separate recent developments ([Millennium announcement](https://millenniumprize.org/news-articles/news/dna-sequencing-pioneers-win-the-2020-millennium-technology-prize/); [Breakthrough announcement](https://breakthroughprize.org/News/65)). The fresh Wolf award strengthens sequencing’s credentials, but its Nobel-eve publicity is not evidence of a last-minute selection mechanism. Nobel expert consultation occurs in March–May, recommendations are written in June–August and submitted in September, before the early-October vote ([official selection process](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/chemistry/)).

The apparent sequencing-symposium advantage is also weaker on inspection. The organizer’s account of the 2025 meeting describes optical pooled screening, rather than a focused appraisal of the original sequencing-by-synthesis invention ([organizer’s report](https://elflab.icm.uu.se/posts/nobel-2025/)). And synthesis during sequencing does not itself mean recognition of Caruthers’s phosphoramidite chemistry, which is a separate enabling technology ([Broad Institute account](https://www.broadinstitute.org/news/inaugural-merkin-prize-biomedical-technology-awarded-dr-marvin-caruthers-developing-technology)). These distinctions explain the small combined-option probabilities: overlapping labels must not become extra chances to capture the same announcement.

## Uncertainties
The largest missing evidence is the committee’s comparison of nominees. Nominations, evaluations and deliberations remain confidential for 50 years. I found no authenticated advance Chemistry result in the material reviewed; that is a search finding, not proof that no leak exists ([confidentiality rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/chemistry/)). A prospective comparison set of equally mature, multiply awarded discoveries—including nonwinners—would improve calibration more than another retrospective list of successful laureates.

The historical precursor audit is name-based, has unequal follow-up and does not independently verify every recipient biography or announcement timestamp. The exact first-publication time of the latest Wolf winners also remains unverified. I therefore avoid turning the award stack into multiplied likelihood ratios. Official wording can still move probability between neighboring options, especially if the prize combines contributions or uses a broad motivation.

The estimate is sensitive to judgmental pooling weights. Holding the other assessments’ relative weights fixed, varying the most concentrated assessment’s weight from 10% to 30% moves sequencing’s probability to roughly 16%–20%. That is model sensitivity, not a statistical confidence interval. The central conclusion survives: sequencing leads, but the evidence does not justify a dominant favorite.
