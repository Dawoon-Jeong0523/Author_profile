## What's non-obvious
The question rewards a discovery match, not a perfect name list. A SAM award including Sagiv, or a dye-cell award including O'Regan, still matches the relevant option. Conversely, adding famous names to a composite DNA or solar option does not make that option more likely to resolve: the motivation must be broad enough to beat the narrower alternative ([resolution and specificity audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/chemistry_2026_forecast_audit.md)). Credit uncertainty and discovery uncertainty are different things.

A prior Nobel in an enabling technique is not an automatic veto on a later platform. Soft ionization and large-scale quantitative proteomics are connected, but they are not identical contributions ([official motivation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2002/summary/); [systems-proteomics attribution](https://www.biochem.mpg.de/matthias-mann-and-wolfgang-baumeister-receive-2026-gairdner-international-awards)). The opposite mistake is letting a career's most-cited papers stand in for a missing discovery: Gray's outside recognition strengthens his case, but it cannot erase the card's lack of direct protein-electron-transfer work under this profile-led framework ([supplied allocation](sandbox:/mnt/data/chemistry_2026_forecast_audit.md); [Clarivate](https://clarivate.com/citation-laureates/chemistry/)).

## Uncertainties
The largest missing evidence is the committee's actual shortlist and motivation. Nominations and deliberative material remain confidential for 50 years, so public accomplishments cannot establish which discoveries reached the final comparison ([selection rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/chemistry/)). Contribution statements would also sharpen laureate allocation: first or last authorship alone does not prove sole conception, and SAMs has a documented four-person credit pool ([Kavli credit record](https://www.kavliprize.org/prizes/nanoscience/2022)).

The cards are descriptive records ending in 2021, not complete discovery histories. The stated attribution anomalies and recent uptake limits remain material even after explicit attenuation ([data coverage and flags](sandbox:/mnt/data/chemistry_2026_forecast_audit.md)). The scoring weights and demographic factors are judgments, not fitted coefficients. In 30,000 score-perturbation tests, proteomics spans roughly 6–12% between the tenth and ninetieth percentiles; this is a model-sensitivity range, not a statistical confidence interval ([sensitivity calculation](sandbox:/mnt/data/chemistry_2026_forecast_audit.md)). The submitted digits preserve normalization, not knowledge at twelve-decimal precision.
