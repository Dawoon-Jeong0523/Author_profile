# Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel: every prize, 1969–2025

Laureates and prize motivations of all 57 prizes (99 laureates), crawled 2026-10-09 from the nobelprize.org list [All prizes in economic sciences](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/all/) and cross-checked against the [Nobel Prize API](https://api.nobelprize.org/2.1/nobelPrizes?nobelPrizeCategory=eco) (CC0), which also gives each laureate's share and affiliation at the time of the prize. Built by `crawl_econ_prizes.py`; tables: `econ_prizes_by_year.csv`, `econ_prizes_laureates.csv`.

- The page says: *The Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel has been awarded 57 times to 99 laureates between 1969 and 2025. Click on the links to get more information.*
- 7 prizes were divided between two works (two motivations in the year); 3 laureates are women.
- **Share** is the laureate's portion of the prize; **motivation** is the official wording; where a year has two works, the overall motivation (if the page gives one) is shown above them.
- **Affiliation** is at the time of the prize (API: institution, city); a laureate can have several; – = none recorded.
