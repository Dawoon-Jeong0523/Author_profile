## TL;DR
Macro leads at 17%, followed by Trade at 16%, Production/Industrial Organization at 14%, and Public/Law/Political Economy at 13% ([forecast calculations](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)). These four fields hold 60% of the probability because long gaps in recognition coincide with mature, distinct research programmes ([assessment and calculations](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)). The forecast selects one primary-JEL field, not every field represented in the laureates’ work ([resolution convention](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

## Context
The announcement is scheduled for October 12, 2026, no earlier than 11:45 CEST, or 09:45 UTC ([announcement schedule](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/about/prize-announcement-dates/)). I apply the stipulated October 9, 2026 information cutoff. The historical coding, committee profiles, and reported paper findings supplied by the client are accepted premises, not independently reconstructed data ([input provenance](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

The event is the single field selected by the specified consensus coding of the official motivation. A divided prize uses its overall motivation where available, otherwise the larger contribution share, then announcement order. The distribution is conditional on a valid field assignment and sums to 100%; there is no separate annulment allocation ([resolution and calculation audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

At the work-field level, the supplied record contains three adjacent-year repetitions across 56 transitions: Equilibrium in 1972→1973, Macro in 1984→1985, and Growth in 2024→2025 ([transition record](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)). That does not establish a fixed rotation policy. The supplied Dolton–Tol findings report an insignificant previous-year-award dummy, and their transition model is an in-sample description rather than validated forecasting performance ([reported study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20767v1)). I therefore apply strong rotation as the requested forecasting design, not as a proven committee rule.

My record-only score combines waiting time, recency tiers, and recent concentration in one prior:

$$
r_i=\frac{e^{0.040w_i}t_i}{1+0.25n_{5i}+0.05(n_{10i}-n_{5i})}.
$$

Here, \(w_i\) is the waiting time, and \(n_{5i}\) and \(n_{10i}\) are the recent share counts. The tier multiplier \(t_i\) is 1.35 for fields last awarded in 2015 or earlier, 1.00 for 2016–2020, 0.55 for 2021–2023, and 0.22 for 2024–2025. These are judgmental settings, not coefficients copied from the paper. Subtracting the five-year count from the ten-year count keeps the concentration windows disjoint. Waiting time and tiers remain related features of one designed prior, not independent evidence ([model specification](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

I normalize these scores, multiply by a mature-work factor, and normalize again. The maturity factors assess research programmes: their importance, accumulated development, distinction from recognized work, and likely primary-code assignment. They do not count coauthors as separate opportunities or treat the paper’s historical candidate pools as living, unawarded inventories ([assessment rules](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

The strongest positive evidence is contribution-specific. The February 25, 2025 recognition of Blanchard, Galí, and Woodford describes a developed monetary-policy framework with substantial central-bank and teaching use. This supports Macro’s remaining scientific inventory, rather than merely its long wait ([BBVA citation](https://www.premiosfronterasdelconocimiento.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/02/press-release-econ-17-frontiers-knowledge-award.pdf)). Empirical IO has similarly direct validation: the February 20, 2018 award to Bresnahan, Pakes, and Porter recognized a coherent programme for measuring competition and market power, including regulatory applications. Its joint nature is a reason to count the programme once, not three times ([BBVA citation](https://www.premiosfronterasdelconocimiento.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2018/02/Press_release_Frontiers_Knowledge_Award_Economics_10th_edition.pdf)).

Equilibrium/Welfare requires a different adjustment. Its classical foundations received substantial prior recognition, but the remaining inventory is not empty. Mas-Colell’s general-equilibrium work, Thomson’s fair-allocation programme, and Roemer–Trannoy’s normative equality-of-opportunity literature establish distinguishable reserves ([general-equilibrium recognition](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/galardonados/andreu-mas-colell/); [fair-allocation publications](https://www.sas.rochester.edu/eco/people/faculty/thomson_william/index.html); [December 2016 review](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jel.20151206)). Sonnenschein’s death on July 15, 2021, and Guesnerie’s death on January 3, 2026, narrow specific packages; they do not eliminate the field’s remaining programmes ([University of Chicago](https://news.uchicago.edu/story/hugo-sonnenschein-memorial-remembering-vision-kindness-uchicago-president-economist); [Collège de France](https://www.college-de-france.fr/fr/actualites/disparition-de-roger-guesnerie)). I use a moderate saturation discount, not a near-total one.

Auxiliary signals receive smaller adjustments. Clarivate’s September 17, 2026 release recognizes Woodford’s monetary-policy work, Athey and Varian’s information-technology economics, and Winter’s organizational research. The Academy also lists a 2026 Nobel Symposium on exchange rates and international capital markets. That is a positive field signal for Trade under the F-code mapping, but it is not evidence of a settled prize choice. The listing supplies a topic and organizer, not a programme, participant list, or link to the selection decision ([Academy listing](https://www.kva.se/en/research-funding/nobel-symposia/)).

Committee coverage changes little. The accepted profiles support modest adjustments for evaluation capacity, particularly in Macro, Public, IO, and Resources. They do not reveal preferences, and I do not assign equal votes to regular members, co-opted members, and the secretary. Societal factors reflect the client’s proposed applications—trade, technology, inequality, and the environment—not a measured attention index ([committee premises and adjustment ledger](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

## What's non-obvious
The option labels are not reliable shortcuts. Voting and social choice coded D71 resolve as Public, despite “social choice” appearing among Equilibrium/Welfare’s illustrative keywords. Network theory coded D85 resolves as Information, not automatically Games. Normative equity coded D63 belongs to Equilibrium/Welfare, while income distribution coded D3 belongs to Labour. The code groups, not familiar field names, drive the outcome ([official JEL definitions](https://www.aeaweb.org/econlit/jelCodes.php?view=econlit); [stipulated mapping](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

The symposium signal also has a narrower meaning than its headline suggests. Exchange rates and international capital markets point directly toward international macroeconomics and finance, which count as Trade here. They do not specifically identify heterogeneous-firm trade as the intended contribution. I raise the field’s weight modestly without turning a conference listing into a candidate shortlist or treating it as an independent confirmation of every trade programme ([Academy listing](https://www.kva.se/en/research-funding/nobel-symposia/)).

## Uncertainties
The strongest assumption is rotation itself. Removing only the recency tiers, while retaining the other ingredients, raises the combined probability of fields last awarded during 2021–2025 from 8% to 19%. The submitted forecast keeps the strong tiers because that is the requested main design. The low recent-field probabilities are therefore conditional on that design, not evidence of a committee prohibition ([structural sensitivity](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

Maturity judgments can reorder the leading fields. A 50,000-draw parameter sensitivity exercise gives central 80% ranges of roughly 13–21% for Macro, 12–19% for Trade, 11–17% for IO, 10–17% for Public, and 6–14% for Equilibrium/Welfare. These are scenario ranges, not calibrated confidence intervals; private committee information and model-form error add uncertainty beyond them ([sensitivity specification and results](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

The missing evidence is a contemporary contribution-level nomination inventory, the committee’s actual shortlist, a comprehensive survival audit, and a validated link between symposium topics and same-year awards. Nominee identities remain confidential for 50 years, so public recognition cannot supply the shortlist ([Nobel nomination rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination?trk=public_post_comment-text)). Dated activity supports surviving representation, but does not certify every illustrative researcher’s eligibility through the cutoff. Societal attention is unmeasured, and committee expertise is not inferred preference ([evidence limits](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).

Finally, the motivation’s wording can move a cross-field contribution between options. I include that risk in the maturity assessments rather than assigning all probability to the researcher’s usual field. The six-decimal JSON values preserve the normalized calculation; they do not imply six-decimal certainty about a secret decision ([exact probability audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/field_forecast_audit.md)).
