Macro leads at 17%, followed by Trade and Production/Industrial Organization at 14% each ([forecast calculation](#evidence)). The supplied record contains three adjacent-year field repeats among 56 transitions: Equilibrium in 1972–1973, Macro in 1984–1985 and Growth in 2024–2025. But the supplied paper summary reports an insignificant previous-year indicator. **Strong rotation is an explicit modeling assumption, not a proven committee rule.** I retain the requested recency penalties without treating another repeat as impossible, and do not use the paper’s sparse, in-sample transition matrix as calibrated forecast probabilities ([client-supplied sequence statistics](#evidence); [Dolton and Tol, March 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20767)).

I construct the record-only weight as `(waiting years + 5)^0.8 × tier / (1 + 0.10 × ten-year prize shares)`. The tier is 1.25 for a last award in 2015 or earlier, 1.00 for 2016–2020, 0.60 for 2021–2023 and 0.40 for 2024–2025. These are my operational settings. Waiting time and the tiers form one rotation score, not independent likelihood ratios. The concentration adjustment is modest, and the nested five-year window is not penalized again ([model assumptions and inputs](#evidence)).

I then multiply by a contribution-maturity factor, M, and normalize. Finally, I apply small supporting factors for societal relevance, S; committee expertise, K; and dated scholarly-recognition or symposium evidence, R, and normalize again. M incorporates distinctness from past awards and likely primary-code placement. S is not measured news volume. K does not assume equal votes or infer preferences from research affiliations. R groups related signals rather than multiplying every medal, citation and mention separately. Historical candidate-pool totals are not used as counts of living, unawarded researchers ([model calculation](#evidence); [paper and its scope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20767)).

Macro has the strongest combination of waiting time and a coherent remaining program. New Keynesian research on nominal rigidities, expectations, stabilization and monetary-policy commitment is mature and distinct from a methods-centered econometrics award or a banking-crisis award. The Clarida–Galí–Gertler synthesis appeared in December 1999 and carries E52/E12 codes. Blanchard, Galí and Woodford received joint BBVA recognition on February 25, 2025; Woodford also received Clarivate recognition in September 2026. I read this as evidence of a clear substantive macro route, not evidence of nomination. The main counterweight is dispersed foundational credit and the recognition of macro-related work under other primary fields ([1999 synthesis](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jel.37.4.1661); [BBVA citation](https://www.premiosfronterasdelconocimiento.es/noticias/premio-fronteras-del-conocimiento-xvii-edicion-economia-finanzas/); [Clarivate economics selections](https://clarivate.com/citation-laureates/Economics/); [supplied historical coding](#evidence)).

Trade’s best residual case is heterogeneous-firm exporting, selection and productivity reallocation, alongside international-finance and trade-policy programs. Melitz’s foundational working paper dates to April 2002. Firm heterogeneity gives a concrete distinction from simply repeating the increasing-returns contribution recognized in the supplied Krugman classification. The opposition is substantive overlap and possible migration into Growth or IO under a different motivation. The Academy lists an international exchange-rate/capital-market symposium for 2026 and an international-trade symposium for 2027. Those are weak supporting signals, not a hidden shortlist; conference proposals have a separate approval process ([Melitz paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w8881); [historical coding](#evidence); [Academy symposium program and process](https://www.kva.se/en/research-funding/nobel-symposia/)).

Production/Industrial Organization receives the largest maturity uplift because structural empirical IO supplies a precise, established contribution: measuring demand, market power, mergers and industry dynamics. The Berry–Levinsohn–Pakes framework was circulated in January 1993 and published in July 1995. It appears in the AEA’s industrial-organization teaching material; Bresnahan, Pakes and Porter received BBVA recognition on February 20, 2018. This is distinct from another award for regulatory theory. The principal opposing ground is classification: estimation-centered wording can resolve as Econometrics. Digital-market and organizational research adds a smaller supporting route, but I do not assign all technology-related recognition automatically to IO ([BLP paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w4264); [AEA reading list](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/cont-ed/ind-org-reading-list); [empirical-IO award citation](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/bbva-foundation-recognizes-bresnahan-pakes-porter-opening-up-field-empirical-industrial-organization/); [Clarivate selections](https://clarivate.com/citation-laureates/Economics/)).

Public, Law, Political Economy has broader remaining work than a blanket institutions-overlap discount suggests. Constitutional accountability and fiscal-policy design, empirically implementable taxation, and economic design of liability are separate mature routes. Persson and Tabellini’s constitutional fiscal-policy paper appeared in March 2004; Saez’s tax-formula paper was circulated in March 2000 and published in 2001. Legal-instrument design supplies another route outside the institutions-growth theme. State-capacity work deserves a larger overlap deduction because the official 2024 explanation also covers political power, credibility and institutional change. Credit allocation across the political-economy program is another constraint: a familiar umbrella of names is not necessarily one uniquely determined contribution ([constitutional fiscal policy](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/000282804322970689); [Saez tax formulas](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7628); [law-and-economics synthesis](https://www.nber.org/papers/w6960); [official 2024 background](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2024/10/popular-economicsciencesprize2024.pdf)).

Equilibrium/Welfare has genuine residual work, so I reject an exhaustion-based collapse of its probability. Geanakoplos and Polemarchakis’s August 1985 incomplete-markets paper supplies a compact contribution concerning constrained inefficiency and welfare improvements. Fleurbaey and Maniquet’s constructive fairness program supplies a separate normative-welfare route. These findings support a milder maturity discount than an assessment based only on old general-equilibrium foundations. The opposing grounds are the narrower verified reserve, earlier recognition of core equilibrium theory, and boundaries that move voting, strategic assignment or implementation into other options. Its long wait keeps it substantial after that discount ([incomplete-markets contribution](https://elischolar.library.yale.edu/cowles-discussion-paper-series/1005/); [fairness and social-welfare synthesis](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/theory-of-fairness-and-social-welfare/57556271063D59B1EF7004FD06BDB327); [normative fair-division synthesis](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262633116/fair-division-and-collective-welfare/); [JEL guide](https://www.aeaweb.org/jel/guide/jel.php)).

Information’s remaining opportunities include network formation, information diffusion, learning and decision-making under uncertainty. ## What's non-obvious
**The resolution rule makes contribution framing as important as disciplinary reputation.** A tax-formula award can be Public while distribution measurement belongs to Labour; voting-based social choice belongs to Public despite the Equilibrium option’s illustrative wording. Monetary-policy work remains Macro even when built in general equilibrium. These are not semantic details: they determine the winning option ([Saez tax paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7628); [monetary-policy synthesis and its E codes](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jel.37.4.1661); [JEL guide](https://www.aeaweb.org/jel/guide/jel.php)).

Digital economics is particularly easy to overcount. Varian’s May 2010 paper carries codes spanning organizational economics, contracts, international business, internet services, advertising and innovation. Thus recognition of information-technology economics is not a clean all-in signal for IO. Conversely, the primary-literature review found precise incomplete-market and fairness contributions that an “equilibrium theory is exhausted” narrative misses. I read both findings as reasons to preserve a broad distribution rather than concentrate on familiar candidate names ([Varian paper and classifications](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.100.2.1); [incomplete-market welfare result](https://elischolar.library.yale.edu/cowles-discussion-paper-series/1005/); [fairness synthesis](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/theory-of-fairness-and-social-welfare/57556271063D59B1EF7004FD06BDB327)).

## Uncertainties
The largest uncertainties concern the assumptions and the unobserved contribution shortlist, not arithmetic.

- Rotation strength matters. Changing the previous-two-years tier from 0.40 to 0.25 or 0.65 moves Growth plus Development/Economic History to approximately 1% or 3%. These are sensitivity scenarios, not statistical confidence intervals; the paper does not validate the strong repeat penalty used here ([sensitivity calculation](#evidence); [Dolton and Tol](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20767)).
- Remaining contribution stock is incompletely measured. Changing Equilibrium/Welfare’s maturity factor from 0.85 to 0.65 or 1.05 moves it to approximately 8% or 12%. Reasonable changes in Macro’s stock assessment can also change first place. A frozen inventory of living, unawarded contributors, with distinct research programs and credit attribution, would narrow this uncertainty. Institutional profiles are not exact-cutoff life certificates, and the historical pool totals do not provide that inventory ([sensitivity calculation](#evidence); [paper’s historical scope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20767)).
- Nominations and motivation wording are hidden. Nomination information remains confidential for 50 years. A divided prize, a narrower contribution or a different primary JEL code can move probability between neighboring options. The listed names illustrate research programs; they are not a ranked or verified nomination list ([official confidentiality rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination); [classification guide](https://www.aeaweb.org/jel/guide/jel.php)).

The submitted probabilities condition on a valid classification under the question’s rules. Their decimal precision preserves exact normalization; it does not imply equally precise knowledge of the committee’s choice ([forecast calculation](#evidence)).
