## TL;DR
Production and Industrial Organization is my leading outcome, at 15%. Macro and Econometrics are the closest alternatives, and these three fields account for 41.4% of the probability. The deciding factor is the contribution described in the official motivation, not the winner’s usual research label.

## Context
The announcement is scheduled for Monday, October 12, 2026, no earlier than 11:45 CEST, or 09:45 UTC. This forecast uses information available at October 10, 2026, 17:15 UTC and excludes subsequent winner information ([official announcement schedule](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/about/prize-announcement-dates/)).

I forecast selection of a contribution first, then its resolving field. I apply the client’s rules for the overall motivation, contribution shares and first-named contribution. Public candidate lists are not verified nominations: nomination records remain confidential for 50 years ([Nobel nomination process](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/)).

Some assignments remain contestable. The empirical-macroeconomics award can be read as methods or Macro, while search-friction research can be read as Information or Labour. Mixed awards also require interpretation of the umbrella-motivation rule. These ambiguities are reasons to limit the historical component, not to conceal the choices ([historical motivations](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/)).

Field rotation supplies less predictive power than its apparent fit suggests. Dolton and Tol’s paper, submitted March 21, 2026, estimates a transition structure with 196 possible cells from only 56 adjacent-year observations. One strong-looking specification uses the award year and five subsequent years, so it cannot demonstrate forecasting performance using information available beforehand ([Dolton–Tol paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.20767v1)).

I independently checked simple models on the table above, making 27 one-year-ahead predictions over 1999–2025 using only preceding years. Mean natural-log loss was 2.90 for smoothed frequencies, 2.88 for a weak gap adjustment, 2.62 for smoothed transitions and 2.64 for uniform probabilities; lower is better. The transition model’s small advantage changes with disputed historical labels. I therefore use no strong rotation multiplier. These are my calculations from the historical proxy, not a replication of the paper’s fitted model ([underlying award motivations](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/)).

Industrial organization has the strongest combined contribution case. The BBVA recognition of Bresnahan, Pakes and Porter packages their research around market power, competition, mergers and market outcomes. That is a substantive IO contribution, even though econometric tools make it possible. A separate recognition of Berry, Levinsohn and Pakes emphasizes demand estimation, which supplies a genuine Econometrics branch rather than a near-certain IO classification ([BBVA contribution account](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/galardonados/ariel-pakes-2/); [Yale announcement, September 23, 2020](https://som.yale.edu/news/2020/09/prof-james-levinsohn-honored-for-oft-cited-1995-paper)).

Digital economics adds another route into IO. Clarivate’s September 17, 2026 selection recognizes Athey and Varian’s information-technology economics, Woodford’s monetary-policy work and Winter’s evolutionary work on organizations. I read these as evidence of mature contributions, not privileged information about the Nobel committee. Clarivate explicitly says its program is not designed to predict winners in a particular year ([Clarivate’s dated announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/clarivate-reveals-citation-laureates-2026-recognizing-transformative-scientific-breakthroughs-302880553.html)).

Macro has the cleanest classification among the leading scenarios. The BBVA announcement of February 25, 2025 recognizes Blanchard, Galí and Woodford for New Keynesian macroeconomics and stabilization policy. Northwestern’s May 30, 2024 announcement separately recognizes Woodford’s bridge between monetary theory and practice. These honors support a coherent contribution package, but they share an underlying scholarly achievement and should not be multiplied as independent selection signals ([BBVA announcement](https://www.premiosfronterasdelconocimiento.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/02/press-release-econ-17-frontiers-knowledge-award.pdf); [Northwestern announcement](https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2024/05/northwestern-announces-nemmers-prize-winners?fj=1)).

Econometrics has several distinct routes. Newey’s Nemmers recognition, reported May 21, 2026, concerns semiparametric econometrics and modern inference. Manski’s BBVA recognition, announced February 25, 2026, concerns credible conclusions from incomplete evidence. Blundell supplies a household-analysis route that can instead resolve as Labour. This breadth keeps Econometrics close to the leaders ([MIT announcement](https://news.mit.edu/2026/whitney-newey-erwin-plein-nemmers-prize-economics-0521); [Manski announcement](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/18th-edition-frontiers-of-knowledge-award-economics-finance-management-charles-manski/); [Blundell’s award citation](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/)).

Precursor awards establish stature better than timing. My cross-check of every economics Nemmers recipient from 1994 through 2024 gives 16 recipients: ten had won the Nobel by the end of 2025, but only two won in their Nemmers award year. This is a censored cohort comparison, not a calibrated same-year selection probability ([complete Nemmers recipient list](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/); [Nobel recipient archive](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/)).

The alternatives are substantial. Autor and Katz’s wage-and-inequality contribution supports Labour. Jackson’s network research supports Information, while the game-theory and decision-theory work recognized in the Rubinstein and Kreps citations supports both Games and Information. Fehr’s established work on fairness, reciprocity and social preferences keeps Behavioural competitive ([Clarivate, September 25, 2025](https://clarivate.com/news/clarivate-unveils-citation-laureates-2025/); [Jackson recognition](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/945094); [Nemmers citations](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/); [University of Zurich research account, July 2026](https://www.ubscenter.uzh.ch/en/news_events/insights/2026-07-how-does-society-shape-the-preferences-of-individuals.html)).

Trade receives a modest institutional nudge. The Academy lists a symposium on exchange rates and international capital markets for 2026 and international trade for 2027. I could not verify the former’s participant program or whether it had occurred by the cutoff. A conference theme is not a shortlist. The public committee roster likewise establishes membership, not preferences; I assign no large committee-specialization adjustment ([Academy symposium listing](https://www.kva.se/en/research-funding/nobel-symposia/); [official committee roster](https://www.nobelprize.org/organization/the-economic-sciences-prize-committee/)).

Political economy and environmental economics remain credible alternatives through the contributions recognized in the Besley–Persson–Tabellini and Dasgupta awards. Their motivations can cross Public, Development, Growth, Resources and Welfare boundaries. Growth receives a low selection score because another growth-centered contribution overlaps the institutional-prosperity and innovation-driven-growth themes already recognized; that is my judgment, not a rule against repetition ([political-economy recognition](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/15th-edition-economics-finance-management-timothy-besley-torsten-persson-guido-tabellini/); [Dasgupta recognition](https://www.bbva.com/en/sustainability/the-bbva-foundation-honors-partha-dasgupta-a-pioneer-in-environmental-economics/); [recent official motivations](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/)).

For the numerical forecast, I weighted hypothetical contribution themes, then allocated each across possible resolving fields. The largest selection weights were empirical IO, monetary macroeconomics, digital economics, behavioural research and econometric inference. Joint-award configurations were grouped rather than counted as independent candidates. The mapped scores appear in the table. My final blend is:

$$
P_f = 0.75\frac{S_f}{127.5} + 0.15\frac{n_f}{57} + 0.10\frac{1}{14}.
$$

Here, \(S_f\) is the contribution score and \(n_f\) is the provisional historical count for field \(f\). The contribution assessment receives 75% of the weight, historical frequencies 15%, and a uniform uncertainty reserve 10%. These weights are judgment calls. Prediction-market prices and reader polls are not quantitative inputs to this calculation.

## What's non-obvious
**Information technology is not Information economics.** Digital industries can resolve through L-codes, advertising auctions through D44, and data-analysis methods through C-codes. Varian’s big-data article is explicitly classified C55, while the Athey–Ellison sponsored-search paper lists D44, L86 and M37. Thus a correct Athey–Varian prediction can still produce an incorrect field prediction ([Varian article and classification](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.28.2.3); [Athey–Ellison working paper](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w15253/w15253.pdf)).

The apparent anti-repeat pattern also depends on coding. Input-output models belong to C67, within this question’s Equilibrium category, and national accounts belong to E01, within Macro. Those distinctions change which historical successions count as repeats. My proxy contains three same-field transitions, rather than treating almost every adjacent award as a different field. That weakens the claim that a field becomes a strong favorite simply because it is overdue ([AEA code definitions](https://www.aeaweb.org/econlit/jelCodes.php?view=jel); [historical motivations used in my count](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/)).

## Uncertainties
The largest missing evidence is the committee’s actual shortlist and contribution-credit assessment. Public honors identify important work, but do not reveal which work the committee selected. Confidential nominations prevent verification of current nomination claims ([Nobel confidentiality rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination/)).

The exact historical consensus codes and the resolver’s model versions and prompts were not verified. Nor did I verify an original level-three JEL declaration for the foundational BLP paper. An archived resolver dataset would improve the historical comparison; the official motivation will settle the main classification ambiguity. I retain substantial Econometrics probability for methods-centered IO recognition rather than inventing a unique original code ([BLP contribution description](https://som.yale.edu/news/2020/09/prof-james-levinsohn-honored-for-oft-cited-1995-paper)).

The leading fields are close enough that small judgment changes reverse their order. In this model, transferring 20 percentage points of the empirical-IO theme’s classification allocation between IO and Econometrics moves each final field probability by about 2.5 percentage points. Varying the main IO and monetary-theme selection weights by one quarter produces roughly 12–17% ranges for either leading field. These are model-sensitivity ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. The JSON decimals preserve the calculation; they do not imply comparable precision in the underlying judgment.
