## Representative works

- `[A O G]` *Income and Wealth Heterogeneity in the Macroeconomy* (1998, Journal of Political Economy); with Anthony A. Smith Jr. — Introduced the Krusell–Smith approximate-aggregation method for incomplete-markets models with aggregate risk. It became the workhorse of heterogeneous-agent macroeconomics.
- `[A O G]` *Capital-Skill Complementarity and Inequality: A Macroeconomic Analysis* (2000, Econometrica); with Lee E. Ohanian, José-Víctor Ríos-Rull, Giovanni L. Violante — Showed that falling equipment prices combined with capital-skill complementarity can account for much of the rise in the US skill premium.
- `[A O G]` *Optimal Taxes on Fossil Fuel in General Equilibrium* (2014, Econometrica); with Mikhail Golosov, John Hassler, Aleh Tsyvinski — Derived a simple closed-form optimal carbon tax formula in a dynamic general-equilibrium climate-economy model.
- `[A · ·]` *Long-Run Implications of Investment-Specific Technological Change* (1997, American Economic Review); with Jeremy Greenwood, Zvi Hercowitz — Quantified the role of investment-specific technical change, measured through relative equipment prices, in postwar US growth.
- `[A · ·]` *Vested Interests in a Positive Theory of Stagnation and Growth* (1996, Review of Economic Studies); with José-Víctor Ríos-Rull — Political-economy growth model in which vested interests in existing technologies block adoption of new ones.
- `[· O ·]` *On the Size of U.S. Government: Political Economy in the Neoclassical Growth Model* (1999, American Economic Review); with José-Víctor Ríos-Rull — Embeds voting and redistribution in a neoclassical growth model to study the political-economy determinants of government size.
- `[· O ·]` *Equilibrium Welfare and Government Policy with Quasi-Geometric Discounting* (2002, Journal of Economic Theory); with Burhanettin Kuruscu, Anthony A. Smith Jr. — Studies welfare and policy when agents have present-biased preferences.
- `[A · ·]` *Consumption-Savings Decisions with Quasi-Geometric Discounting* (2003, Econometrica); with Anthony A. Smith Jr. — Analyzed equilibria and indeterminacy in savings problems with time-inconsistent (quasi-hyperbolic) preferences.
- `[· O ·]` *Revisiting the Welfare Effects of Eliminating Business Cycles* (2009, Review of Economic Dynamics); with Toshihiko Mukoyama, Ayşegül Şahin — Reassesses the welfare cost of business cycles in a model with idiosyncratic risk and labor-market elements, showing that heterogeneity matters.
- `[A · ·]` *Is Piketty's 'Second Law of Capitalism' Fundamental?* (2015, Journal of Political Economy); with Anthony A. Smith Jr. — Argued from standard growth theory that capital-income ratios need not explode as growth slows.
- `[A · ·]` *Gross Worker Flows over the Business Cycle* (2017, American Economic Review); with Toshihiko Mukoyama, Richard Rogerson, Ayşegül Şahin — Model of flows among employment, unemployment and nonparticipation that matches cyclical labor-market dynamics.
- `[· · G]` *Labor Supply in the Past, Present, and Future: A Balanced-Growth Perspective* (2020, Journal of Political Economy); with Timo Boppart — Growth model with preferences that generate a secular decline in hours worked, consistent with historical data.
- `[· O ·]` *Directed Technical Change as a Response to Natural Resource Scarcity* (2021, Journal of Political Economy); with John Hassler, Conny Olovsson — Shows how natural-resource scarcity directs innovation toward resource-saving technologies, linking growth theory, energy and environmental macro.
