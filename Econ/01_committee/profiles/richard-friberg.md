- `[· O G]` *In which currency should exporters set their prices?* (1998, Journal of International Economics) — Theoretical model of exporters' invoicing-currency choice under exchange-rate uncertainty, linking it to market structure, demand and risk exposure.
- `[A O ·]` *Two monies, two markets? Variability and the option to segment* (2001, Journal of International Economics) — Analyzes how separate currencies and exchange-rate variability shape firms' incentives to segment international markets, and how this affects arbitrage and pricing.
