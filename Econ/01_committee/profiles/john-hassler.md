- `[A O ·]` *Directed Technical Change as a Response to Natural Resource Scarcity* (2021, Journal of Political Economy); with Per Krusell, Conny Olovsson — Estimates energy–capital/labor complementarity and models endogenous energy-saving technical change. - `[A O G]` *The Survival of the Welfare State* (2003, American Economic Review); with José V. - `[A O ·]` *Intelligence, Social Mobility, and Growth* (2000, American Economic Review); with José V. Rodríguez Mora — Links ability, social mobility and the allocation of talent to growth, contrasting returns to innate ability with inherited advantage.

Rodríguez Mora, Kjetil Storesletten, Fabrizio Zilibotti — Shows how unemployment insurance and geographic mobility jointly determine political equilibria, helping explain persistent differences between Europe and the US.
- `[A · ·]` *Economics and Climate Change: Integrated Assessment in a Multi-Region World* (2012, Journal of the European Economic Association); with Per Krusell — Multi-region integrated assessment model of the distributional effects of climate change and of alternative carbon-tax designs across regions and oil producers.
- `[· O ·]` *Equilibrium Unemployment Insurance* (1999, Journal of Political Economy); with José V. Rodríguez Mora — Political-economy / dynamic-equilibrium account of unemployment insurance as an endogenous institution.
- `[· O ·]` *Climate Policy* (2018, Annual Review of Economics); with Per Krusell, Conny Olovsson — Survey of economic approaches to climate policy: carbon pricing, dynamic incentives, technology and macroeconomic modeling.
