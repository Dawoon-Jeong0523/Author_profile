## Representative works

- `[A O ·]` *Conflicts of Interest and Market Illiquidity in Bankruptcy Auctions: Theory and Tests* (2000, Journal of Finance) — Theory and tests using Swedish auction-based bankruptcy data. Shows frequent salebacks to former owners, consistent with market illiquidity and creditor conflicts of interest.
- `[A O G]` *Financial Contracting Theory Meets the Real World: An Empirical Analysis of Venture Capital Contracts* (2003, Review of Economic Studies); with Steven N. Kaplan — Documents how actual VC contracts separately allocate cash-flow, control, voting, board and liquidation rights, often contingent on performance. This is consistent with incomplete-contracting and agency theories.
- `[A O G]` *Characteristics, Contracts, and Actions: Evidence from Venture Capitalist Analyses* (2004, Journal of Finance); with Steven N. Kaplan — Uses VCs' internal investment memos to link perceived risks to contract terms. Assesses the roles of screening, contracting and monitoring in resolving agency problems.
- `[A O G]` *Leveraged Buyouts and Private Equity* (2009, Journal of Economic Perspectives); with Steven N. Kaplan — Widely cited synthesis of how PE creates value through financial, governance and operational engineering. Also reviews evidence on returns and boom-bust cycles.
- `[A O ·]` *Why Are Buyouts Levered? The Financial Structure of Private Equity Funds* (2009, Journal of Finance); with Ulf Axelson, Michael S. Weisbach — Theory explaining fund-level equity combined with deal-level debt in PE as a response to agency problems between GPs and LPs.
- `[A · ·]` *Which CEO Characteristics and Abilities Matter?* (2012, Journal of Finance); with Steven N. Kaplan, Mark M. Klebanov, Morten Sorensen — Uses detailed assessments of CEO candidates in buyout and VC deals to relate personality and ability traits to selection and performance.
- `[A O ·]` *Borrow Cheap, Buy High? The Determinants of Leverage and Pricing in Buyouts* (2013, Journal of Finance); with Ulf Axelson, Tim Jenkinson, Michael S. Weisbach — Shows that buyout leverage tracks credit-market conditions rather than firm-level determinants, and that higher leverage goes with higher prices.
- `[A O ·]` *Private Equity and Industry Performance* (2017, Management Science); with Shai Bernstein, Josh Lerner, Morten Sørensen — Finds that industries with more PE activity grow faster and are less volatile, without evidence of negative spillovers.
- `[· O ·]` *Private Equity and Financial Fragility during the Crisis* (2019, Review of Financial Studies); with Shai Bernstein, Josh Lerner, Morten Sørensen — Studies how PE-backed firms fared during the financial crisis, bearing on whether PE ownership increases vulnerability or improves resilience.
- `[· · G]` *Since you're so rich, you must be really smart: Talent and the finance wage premium*; with Michael Böhm, Daniel Metzger — Uses Swedish registry data to argue that the rising finance-sector wage premium is largely driven by increased sorting of cognitive and non-cognitive talent into finance.
