## Representative works

- `[A O G]` *Long-Term Effects of Class Size* (2013, Quarterly Journal of Economics); with Björn Öckert, Hessel Oosterbeek — Uses maximum class-size rules in Swedish schools and administrative data (regression discontinuity). Finds that smaller primary-school classes raise cognitive and noncognitive skills, educational attainment and adult earnings.
- `[A O G]` *Optimal Unemployment Insurance in Search Equilibrium* (2001, Journal of Labor Economics); with Bertil Holmlund — Analyzes UI design in an equilibrium search framework and shows that benefits declining over the unemployment spell can be welfare-improving.
- `[A O ·]` *Improving Incentives in Unemployment Insurance: A Review of Recent Research* (2006, Journal of Economic Surveys); with Bertil Holmlund — Reviews theory and evidence on UI design that preserves insurance while strengthening search incentives, including benefit duration, monitoring and sanctions, and workfare.
- `[A O ·]` *Dynamic Treatment Assignment: The Consequences for Evaluations Using Observational Data* (2008, Journal of Business & Economic Statistics); with Per Johansson — Shows how dynamic program assignment and treatment timing complicate evaluation with observational data and proposes an appropriate estimator. Influential for labor-market program evaluation.
- `[A O ·]` *Life-Cycle Effects of Age at School Start* (2014, The Economic Journal); with Björn Öckert — Exploits birthdate-based school-entry rules in Sweden to estimate effects of school starting age on schooling, labor-market entry and lifetime earnings.
- `[A O ·]` *Inside the Black Box of Class Size: Mechanisms, Behavioral Responses, and Social Background* (2016 (A) / 2020 (O), Journal of Public Economics (A) / Journal of Human Resources (O)); with Björn Öckert, Hessel Oosterbeek — Studies the mechanisms behind class-size effects, including parental and teacher responses and heterogeneity by social background.

- `[· O ·]` *Parental Responses to Public Investments in Children: Evidence from a Maximum Class Size Rule* (2016, Journal of Human Resources); with Hessel Oosterbeek, Björn Öckert — Uses class-size rules to examine whether parents offset or reinforce public school investments.
- `[A · ·]` *Mismatch of Talent: Evidence on Match Quality, Entry Wages, and Job Mobility* (2018, American Economic Review); with Lena Hensvik, Oskar Nordström Skans — Measures worker–job match quality from multidimensional skill data.
