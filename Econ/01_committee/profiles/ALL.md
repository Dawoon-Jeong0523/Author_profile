## Tommy Andersson (member)

Department of Economics, Lund University · [full profile](tommy-andersson.md)

An applied microeconomic theorist and market designer. He combines rigorous axiomatic mechanism design with attention to whether mechanisms solve real allocation problems where prices are unavailable, unacceptable or incomplete. Examples include kidney exchange, refugee and housing placement, and other public-sector matching.
