# Step 7: the people question (Preseen)

Step 7a (10 October 2026): [`people_candidate_prompts.ipynb`](people_candidate_prompts.ipynb) writes the Preseen
context notes for the people question: the candidate combinations of the five fields of the pool with their defining
works, and the context the user chose for choosing among them. Sources: the step-6 integrated lists
(`../06_candidates/committee/<field>/integrated.json`) and pool (30 candidates, 7/7/6/6/4 in proportion to the latest
field forecast), the latest field run (`../04_field_forecast/preseen_exp/fields14_nobel/runs.csv`, main arm), the
step-5 age paragraph (`../05_laureates/prompt/01_age_at_award.md`) and the coded prize record
(`../04_field_forecast/results/prize_works_fields14.csv`). The notebook is generated by
[`make_people_nb.py`](make_people_nb.py) and executed with
`jupyter nbconvert --to notebook --execute --inplace --ExecutePreprocessor.kernel_name=curvature people_candidate_prompts.ipynb`
(about 10 s, no paid calls, nothing uploaded).

| Note (upload order) | Content |
|---|---|
| `context/00_instruction.md` | the brief: goal (probabilities over the 30 options, conditional, sum 100 %), how to use notes 01-25, eligibility, required output (grounds per option; field totals vs note 01) |
| `context/01_field_forecast.md` | the latest field forecast (14 fields), the five of the pool rescaled to 100 %, candidates per field |
| `context/02_age_and_timing.md` | the age record of the 99 laureates (step 5) and how to read the stated birth years |
| `context/03_awarded_prizes.md` | every prize of the five fields 1969-2025, and the other fields' prizes since 2000 |
| `context/11_candidates_<field>.md` ... `15_...` | per field: people with birth years, contribution [JEL], up to 4 defining works; one provenance sentence |
| `context/21_committee_<field>.md` ... `25_...` | per field: committee score (of 15) with points per model, members, nominations, per-person support, other people named, people named but not eligible, the committee's reasoning incl. reservations; the next 5 candidates outside the pool |

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `questions/people30.json` | the question: "Which contribution will the 2026 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences be awarded for?", 30 options "<contribution> — <people> (<field>)", no Other (conditional, annulled if none matches), resolution by contribution (larger share / named first for divided prizes; most laureates, then most specific, if several match) |
| `questions/people30_other.json` | the same with a final "Other" option (comprehensive) |
| `run_people.sh` | submission (not run): arms control (no notes) and main (the 14 notes, assume_true); refuses unless context/ has 14 notes and living_check.csv exists; state in preseen_exp/<sub>/ |

Eligibility screen (not a note): [`check_living_econ.py`](check_living_econ.py) looks up every shown person on
Wikidata (reusing `../../experiment/preseen/check_living.py` with an occupation filter for economists and legal
scholars) -> `living_check.csv`, cache `living_cache.json`; the notebook's section 1 lists the rows to look at.

Knobs (first code cell): `POOL`, `MAX_WORKS` (4), `ORDER` (`committee` | `alphabetical`), `SHOW_BORN` (True),
`FIELD_PCT` (True), `PROVENANCE` (True), `AWARDED_OTHER_SINCE` (2000), `OTHERS_SHOWN` (5).

Not used: external signals (prediction markets, other prizes), as at the field stage; collaboration networks from the
author-profile pipeline (considered on 10 October, dropped by the user for time).

committee notes' effect).
