## TL;DR
BLP demand and market-power estimation leads my forecast at 18%, followed by New Keynesian monetary-policy foundations at 17% ([BLP recognition](https://ir.clarivate.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2020/Clarivate-Reveals-2020-Citation-Laureates---Annual-List-of-Researchers-of-Nobel-Class/default.aspx); [monetary-policy recognition](https://clarivate.com/citation-laureates/Economics/)). Firm heterogeneity in international trade ranks third at 9% ([Melitz recognition](https://www.economics.harvard.edu/news/professor-marc-melitz-awarded-2023-onassis-prize?page=1)). These probabilities are conditional on a listed contribution matching the official motivation, not on the named people forming the exact laureate combination.

## Evidence
The historical backbone is small: 57 economics prizes went to 99 laureates from 1969 through 2025. That supports broad reference classes, not a reliable statistical fit for this particular contribution menu ([official historical record](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/?os=qtft_1)). Nominations remain confidential for 50 years, so public candidate lists are not verified shortlists ([nomination rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination?trk=public_post_comment-text)). ([nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/?os=qtft_1))

Among those ten conversions, the delay ranged from the same year to 16 years, with a median of seven years. **New Keynesian monetary economics has the strongest recent recognition.** Woodford’s 2024 Nemmers citation explicitly recognizes New Keynesian general-equilibrium analysis and the connection between monetary theory and practice. BBVA’s award announced February 25, 2025 recognized Blanchard, Galí and Woodford for the same research program. Clarivate added Woodford on September 17, 2026 for monetary-policy theory and practice ([Nemmers citation](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/); [BBVA announcement](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/frontiers-knowledge-award-blanchard-gali-woodford-economics-17-edition/); [Clarivate announcement](https://ir.clarivate.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Clarivate-Reveals-Citation-Laureates-2026-Recognizing-Transformative-Scientific-Breakthroughs/)). I treat these as overlapping recognition of scholarly standing, not three independent signals about the Nobel decision. Clarivate itself disclaims prediction of a particular year’s recipients. ([nemmers.northwestern.edu](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/))

BLP has the clearest contribution-specific recognition. Clarivate’s September 23, 2020 announcement names Berry, Levinsohn and Pakes specifically for their random-coefficients demand-estimation model. Pakes’s 2022 Nemmers adds broader empirical-IO recognition ([Clarivate citation](https://ir.clarivate.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2020/Clarivate-Reveals-2020-Citation-Laureates---Annual-List-of-Researchers-of-Nobel-Class/default.aspx); [Nemmers citation](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/)). But the BBVA award announced February 20, 2018 went to Bresnahan, Pakes and Porter for empirical industrial organization broadly—not to the BLP trio. I therefore reserve probability for entry and competition rather than assigning every empirical-IO signal to BLP ([BBVA release](https://www.premiosfronterasdelconocimiento.es/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2018/02/Press_release_Frontiers_Knowledge_Award_Economics_10th_edition.pdf)). Trade has several distinct routes. I read the former as a modest positive for Obstfeld–Rogoff, not a candidate-specific timing signal; the published register supplies no participant program establishing that connection ([Academy register](https://www.kva.se/en/research-funding/nobel-symposia/)). ([economics.harvard.edu](https://www.economics.harvard.edu/news/professor-marc-melitz-awarded-2023-onassis-prize?page=1))

Recent awards create proximity penalties, not exclusions. The 2022 banking and financial-crisis prize is adjacent to collateral-driven credit cycles; the 2024 institutions prize is adjacent to political institutions and legal origins; and the 2025 innovation-growth prize is adjacent to Grossman–Helpman’s growth work ([2022 motivation](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2022/10/press-economicsciencesprize2022.pdf); [2024 motivation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2024/robinson/facts/); [2025 motivations](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/lists/all-prizes-in-economic-sciences/?os=qtft_1)). A growth prize does not rule out monetary economics or Barro’s fiscal contribution. Kiyotaki–Moore also retains direct recognition for amplification through financial imperfections ([BBVA announcement, March 4, 2021](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/the-frontiers-of-knowledge-award-goes-to-bernanke-gertler-kiyotaki-and-moore-for-establishing-the-nature-of-the-linkage-between-the-financial-sector-and-the-real-economy-and-how-it-operates/)).

Public economics and welfare remain credible alternatives. Saez’s 2009 Clark citation directly supports connecting optimal taxation to measurable parameters, while Finkelstein’s 2012 citation supports empirical insurance analysis ([Saez citation](https://www.aeaweb.org/about-aea/honors-awards/bates-clark/emmanuel-saez); [Finkelstein citation](https://www.aeaweb.org/about-aea/honors-awards/bates-clark/amy-finkelstein)). Dasgupta’s BBVA recognition announced February 28, 2024 directly supports natural capital, green accounting and sustainability. That makes sustainability my leading welfare option ([BBVA announcement](https://www.bbva.com/en/sustainability/the-bbva-foundation-honors-partha-dasgupta-a-pioneer-in-environmental-economics/)).

For the calculation, I used subjective conditional field priors of 32.0% for macro, 24% each for trade and industrial organization, 12% for public/law/political economy, and 8% for equilibrium/welfare. Within fields, I assessed maturity, contribution boundaries and award readiness. Evidence adjustments were deliberately modest: the largest upward weights went to New Keynesian monetary economics and BLP; neighboring prizes received small discounts. These are judgment inputs, not estimated historical frequencies.

The independent evidence model was mixed back toward its baseline to limit overconfidence, then given 80% weight in the final estimate. The remaining 20% came from an equal-weight pool of the retained contribution-level assessments. I excluded the heavily market/poll-led numerical estimate from that pool and did not transfer its candidate-market percentages into contribution probabilities. The calculation is:

$$
p_i=0.80\left(0.85\frac{b_i m_i}{\sum_j b_jm_j}+0.15b_i\right)+0.20r_i.
$$

Here, \(b_i\) is the conditional baseline, \(m_i\) the judgmental evidence adjustment, and \(r_i\) the pooled assessment. The numerical field totals are approximately 33.5% macro, 24% industrial organization, 23% trade, 11% public/law/political economy, and 8% equilibrium/welfare. Decimal rounding was reconciled so the JSON probabilities sum exactly to one.

## What's non-obvious
**Friberg’s committee appointment is not a clean BLP signal.** His March 2026 CV dates service to 2025 and lists industrial organization and trade; the official roster identifies him as co-opted ([CV](https://staffstream.hhs.se/public/streamdocument.ashx?dl=00119_002); [committee roster](https://www.nobelprize.org/organization/the-economic-sciences-prize-committee/)). His research also includes an April 2026 paper on menu costs and nominal price adjustment with committee chair John Hassler ([institutional publication record](https://research.hhs.se/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Menu-Costs-and-Asymmetric-Price-Adjustment/991001678999306056?institution=46SSOE_INST)). Moreover, a recent field-rotation paper codes Friberg as a labor economist, contrary to his own CV. That discrepancy prevents me from treating its committee-based model outputs as calibrated probabilities ([paper, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.20767v1)).

Contribution matching removes several weak exclusions. An award to Blanchard, Galí and Woodford could match the New Keynesian option despite the listed names differing ([matched recognition](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/frontiers-knowledge-award-blanchard-gali-woodford-economics-17-edition/)). Eaton’s death on February 9, 2024 changes recipient availability, not whether Kortum can be recognized for their joint contribution ([Yale-hosted obituary](https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/campuspress.yale.edu/dist/c/4257/files/2024/08/J.-Eaton-Obituary.pdf)). Likewise, Krusell’s role as secretary creates an attribution and process question for heterogeneous-agent macroeconomics, because he coauthored the aggregate-shocks method with Smith; it does not establish exclusion of the entire contribution ([committee roster](https://www.nobelprize.org/organization/the-economic-sciences-prize-committee/); [original paper](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/250034)).

## Uncertainties
The largest gap is the confidential shortlist and the committee’s intended motivation. A broad monetary-policy or empirical-IO award could cross option boundaries, and a divided award would make the question’s share and ordering rules decisive. The narrow numerical lead of BLP over New Keynesian economics is not a strong ranking advantage.

I could not verify a candidate-specific symposium program or recusal arrangement. Those would sharpen the international-macro and heterogeneous-agent assessments; their absence from the retrieved record is not negative evidence. Finally, an October 10 interview reports that recommendations are often settled by June, which makes late publicity a weak timing signal ([committee-process reporting](https://efn.se/bakom-kulisserna-pa-nobelpriset-i-ekonomi)). The six-decimal probabilities preserve the calculation, not six-decimal knowledge of the committee’s choice.
