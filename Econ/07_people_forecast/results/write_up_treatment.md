## TL;DR
The structural demand and market-power contribution associated with Berry, Levinsohn and Pakes leads at 17% ([calculation audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)). New Keynesian monetary-policy analysis follows at 13%, and heterogeneous-firm trade follows at 8% ([calculation audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)). These probabilities are conditional on a listed contribution matching the prize; they do not require the exact named laureate lineup ([resolution rules](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

## Context
The announcement is scheduled for Monday, 12 October 2026, no earlier than 11:45 CEST, or 09:45 UTC ([Royal Swedish Academy schedule](https://www.kva.se/nyheter/hostens-offentliggoranden-av-nobel-och-ekonomipris-faststallda/)). This forecast is prepared as of 10 October 2026 at 20:20 UTC. Substantive external evidence is restricted to material available by 9 October; the client’s field forecast dated 10 October is accepted as a separate premise ([scope and inputs](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

The question forecasts the recognized contribution, not simply who wins. A different laureate combination can match an option, while a familiar name winning for different work does not automatically match it. The distribution excludes annulment and allocates all probability among the listed contributions ([resolution rules](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

## Evidence
The historical backbone is the client’s full award and age record for 1969–2025, covering 99 laureates, with a 9 October 2026 information vintage. Median award age was 67; the middle 80% were aged 57–78. Only 4 laureates were below 55, and 6 were at least 80 ([supplied historical record](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)). These are ages among winners, not annual winning rates for candidates. I therefore use age as a soft adjustment at the extremes, not as a mechanical selection formula. Contribution maturity and attribution matter more within the usual age range.

The supplied field forecast already incorporates field rotation. I do not apply a second bonus for how long a field has waited. Instead, I adjust the starting weights for the strength and coverage of the actual listed contributions. The simulated committee supplies arguments to examine, not observations of the real committee: its 33 ballots per field are correlated model outputs, and its scores are not probabilities ([input treatment](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

BLP has the strongest compact case. Its original working paper appeared in January 1993 and its canonical journal article in July 1995. It connects differentiated-product demand estimation to an oligopoly-equilibrium framework for estimating costs and market power ([original publication record](https://www.nber.org/papers/w4264)). Its distinguishing achievement is not inventing discrete choice or heterogeneous preferences. The official background for McFadden’s earlier prize already covers mixed logit and simulation methods ([Academy’s 2000 background](https://www.kva.se/app/uploads/2000/10/globalassets-priser-nobel-2000-scibackeken00-1.pdf)). I read BLP’s handling of endogenous prices, market-level information and equilibrium counterfactuals as a distinct contribution.

The recognition is also contribution-specific. Berry, Levinsohn and Pakes were jointly recognized as Citation Laureates on 23 September 2020 for their demand-estimation work ([Yale announcement](https://som.yale.edu/news/2020/09/prof-james-levinsohn-honored-for-oft-cited-1995-paper)). Pakes’s 2022 Nemmers citation recognizes a broader empirical-IO portfolio, including market power, mergers and productivity ([Northwestern citations](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/)). This supports readiness, but it also warns against treating every empirical-IO award as a BLP award.

New Keynesian analysis has the strongest recent recognition cluster. Woodford received the 2024 Nemmers Prize for advancing that framework ([Northwestern citation](https://nemmers.northwestern.edu/past-winners/)). BBVA’s announcement on 25 February 2025 recognized Blanchard, Galí and Woodford for the framework and its policy applications ([BBVA announcement](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/frontiers-knowledge-award-blanchard-gali-woodford-economics-17-edition/)). Clarivate subsequently recognized Woodford’s monetary-policy contributions in its 2026 cohort ([economics citation](https://clarivate.com/citation-laureates/Economics/)). These signals concern substantially overlapping work, so I do not count them as independent evidence of a shortlist. Clarivate’s announcement, dated 17 September 2026, explicitly says its program does not predict recipients in a particular year ([program caveat](https://ir.clarivate.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Clarivate-Reveals-Citation-Laureates-2026-Recognizing-Transformative-Scientific-Breakthroughs/default.aspx)).

Heterogeneous-firm trade offers a clear substantive mechanism. Melitz’s model explains exporter selection, exit and reallocation toward more productive firms. The original paper presents this as an extension of earlier representative-firm trade theory, not a relabeling of it ([original paper and 2003 publication record](https://www.nber.org/papers/w8881)). That distinction outweighs a large penalty based on Melitz’s relative youth. The harder issue is sharing credit between theory and the empirical exporter literature.

Previous-prize overlap needs close reading. The Academy’s 2022 background describes Kiyotaki–Moore’s asset-price and collateral feedback as an important dynamic-equilibrium mechanism, while the awarded core concerns banking, monitoring and crises ([2022 scientific background](https://www.kva.se/app/uploads/2022/10/scibackeken22.pdf)). This is meaningful adjacency, not proof that collateral cycles have already been awarded. Platform theory faces a stronger obstacle: the 2014 background explicitly discusses Rochet–Tirole’s two-sided-market work and related contributions by Armstrong and Caillaud–Jullien ([2014 scientific background](https://www.kva.se/app/uploads/2014/10/globalassets-priser-ekonomi-2014-scibackeken14.pdf)).

Two older awards also constrain broad motivations. Optimal income-tax theory was part of the official explanation of the 1996 prize, so the strongest new tax case is the bridge to empirically estimable sufficient statistics ([1996 explanation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1996/press-release/?source=post_page---------------------------)). Becker’s 1992 explanation explicitly includes crime and punishment, limiting the novelty of a generic economic-enforcement motivation without exhausting accident liability and the wider analysis of law ([1992 explanation](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/1992/press-release/?print=1)).

Natural-capital accounting is the strongest listed welfare alternative. BBVA’s 28 February 2024 citation recognizes Dasgupta’s sustainability measurement through comprehensive wealth and the social value of nature ([BBVA citation](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/frontiers-knowledge-award-16th-edition-economics-finance-management/)). I regard this as distinguishable from climate modeling alone. It also can match this question even if the Academy describes it as environmental economics rather than welfare economics; the bracketed field is not a resolution gate ([matching rules](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

The recognition evidence is not a clock. The latest Nemmers economics announcement, on 6 May 2026, recognized Newey, and BBVA’s latest economics announcement, on 25 February 2026, recognized Manski—neither contribution is listed here ([Northwestern announcement](https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2026/05/five-top-scholars-receive-the-2026-nemmers-prize); [BBVA announcement](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/18th-edition-frontiers-of-knowledge-award-economics-finance-management-charles-manski/)). I use earlier recognition to assess standing, not to manufacture certainty about this year.

My numerical model has two levels. I multiply the supplied field weights by subjective candidate-readiness adjustments and normalize. Within each field, I allocate 90% according to contribution-merit weights and 10% equally among its listed options. That uncertainty component tempers concentration. The multipliers and merits are judgments, not statistically estimated likelihood ratios; all inputs and arithmetic are preserved in the [calculation audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md).

IO’s increase exceeds five percentage points because BLP combines maturity, identifiable attribution and a distinct empirical method. Public economics’ decrease exceeds five points because two prominent listed packages—constitutions and legal origins—sit close to the recent institutions prize, while the taxation and insurance packages have younger lineups and less compact attribution. Law and economics adds a mature alternative, but its listed founders are at the older extreme. These are weaknesses of this particular pool, not a claim that public economics lacks prize-worthy work ([candidate and award premises; adjustment rationale](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

Macro rises modestly because New Keynesian analysis and collateral cycles provide two mature alternatives. Trade stays close to its prior because heterogeneous firms, quantitative Ricardian trade and open-economy macro offset weaker overlap and attribution cases elsewhere. Welfare also stays close: natural-capital accounting offsets the stronger previous-prize overlap in the mathematical-equilibrium candidates ([candidate assessments and calculations](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

## What's non-obvious
An empirical-IO favorite is not automatically a BLP outcome. BBVA’s broader recognition of Bresnahan, Pakes and Porter covers demand, entry, productivity, dynamics and collusion. Its contribution-level citation distinguishes these research families ([BBVA credit mapping](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/galardonados/timothy-bresnahan-2/)). The future motivation must match structural differentiated-product demand and market-power analysis before the laureate-count tie-breaker matters. Likewise, a monetary-policy prize can match the New Keynesian option with a different lineup, as the Blanchard–Galí–Woodford recognition illustrates ([BBVA monetary-policy citation](https://www.frontiersofknowledgeawards-fbbva.es/noticias/frontiers-knowledge-award-blanchard-gali-woodford-economics-17-edition/)). Contribution-first matching both broadens eligibility and limits how much probability can be transferred from prominent names.

The field labels also conceal overlap. Natural-capital work can enter through an environmental motivation, and collateral work can enter through a financial motivation without invalidating the listed options ([resolution rules](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)). Conversely, the Academy’s symposium listing—international exchange rates and capital markets for 2026, international trade for 2027—does not establish a shortlist or an award timetable ([Academy listing](https://www.kva.se/en/research-funding/nobel-symposia/)). I give it little weight. Institutional proximity and past committee service are not substitutes for evidence of the current committee’s choice.

## Uncertainties
The main missing evidence is the real committee’s contribution ranking. Nominee identities remain confidential for 50 years, and I found no verified public shortlist ([official confidentiality rules](https://www.nobelprize.org/nomination?trk=public_post_comment-text)). The short time before announcement does not remove that uncertainty. The decisive unknown is selection and wording, not whether these established research programs will mature further before the announcement.

The birth years are supplied, unverified inputs, so actual announcement-day ages can be one year below the displayed calendar-year ages. Historical winner ages also lack the denominator needed to estimate age-specific selection rates among all plausible candidates. Better candidate-pool calibration would improve those adjustments. I could not establish symposium participants or a reliable connection between its topic and the award decision; that gap is not evidence against the international-macro candidates ([data limitations](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).

I tested the allocation under independent perturbations to field and contribution weights. Across 100,000 scenarios, the central 90% assumption-sensitivity range was 8%–26% for BLP and 6%–21% for New Keynesian analysis. These are not empirically calibrated confidence intervals ([sensitivity calculation](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)). They show how strongly reasonable judgment changes can move the numbers. The extra decimals in the probability object preserve normalization, not confidence at that precision; no prediction-market prices or public forecast-aggregate weights entered the calculation ([method audit](sandbox:/mnt/data/nobel_2026_forecast_audit.md)).
