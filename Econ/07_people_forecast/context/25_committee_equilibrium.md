# Virtual committee support and reasoning: Equilibrium, Welfare

How to read: the virtual committee is the simulation described in the candidate notes. Each of its 33 ballots (11 personas x 3 models: A = claude-opus-5-5, O = gpt-5.5, G = gemini-3.1-pro) ranked up to five contributions in the field; rank 1 earns 5 points, rank 5 earns 1, each model's points are divided by its number of valid ballots and summed over the three models, so the score is at most 15 (every ballot of every model ranking the candidate first). Members = personas that nominated the candidate on at least one model. The reasoning summarizes the personas' rationales, including their reservations; it is simulated, not the real committee's view.

165 nominations in this field, integrated into 24 candidates; the 4 candidates of the pool hold 58 % of the field's total score.

1. Andreu Mas-Colell, Yves Balasko: for the analysis of the structure, regularity and limits of general equilibrium theory

   Named in its nominations: Andreu Mas-Colell 21; Yves Balasko 3

2. John E. Roemer, Marc Fleurbaey, François Maniquet: for the theory of equality of opportunity, responsibility and fairness in welfare economics
   Support: score 6.91 of 15 (A 2.36, O 1.55, G 3.00); 11 of 11 members; 31 nominations (A 11, O 7, G 13)
   Named in its nominations: John E. Roemer 24; Marc Fleurbaey 24; François Maniquet 7
   Reasoning: They gave operational normative criteria separating circumstance from effort, now used in empirical work on inequality of opportunity and redistribution. The recurring reservation is closeness to Sen's 1998 prize and the difficulty of observing circumstances.

3. John Geanakoplos, Herakles Polemarchakis, Michael Magill: for the theory of general equilibrium with incomplete markets and its constrained inefficiency
   Support: score 6.00 of 15 (A 2.64, O 2.73, G 0.64); 10 of 11 members; 19 nominations (A 9, O 8, G 2)
   Named in its nominations: John Geanakoplos 18; Herakles Polemarchakis 7; Michael Magill 6; also Martine Quinzii 4
   Reasoning: Incomplete-market equilibria are generically constrained inefficient, giving a rigorous foundation for policy intervention, macroprudential reasoning and heterogeneous-agent macro. Reservations are indirect empirical and quantitative links and widely shared credit.

4. Partha Dasgupta, Geoffrey Heal: for the welfare economics of sustainability, natural capital and intergenerational wealth accounting
   Support: score 4.73 of 15 (A 1.73, O 0.45, G 2.55); 10 of 11 members; 17 nominations (A 5, O 2, G 10)
