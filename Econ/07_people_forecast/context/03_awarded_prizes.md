# Prizes already awarded: the five fields of the pool (1969-2025) and the other fields since 2000

Official motivations (nobelprize.org) with the consensus JEL code of the awarded work, as coded by three language
models for this forecast. A candidate whose contribution is the same as, or a re-labelling of, an awarded contribution
is unlikely to be recognized again; a distinct contribution in the same area is possible.

Macro (8 prize works):
- 1974 Gunnar Myrdal; Friedrich von Hayek: "for their pioneering work in the theory of money and economic fluctuations and for their penetrating analysis of the interdependence of economic, social and institutional phenomena" [E32]
- 1976 Milton Friedman: "for his achievements in the fields of consumption analysis, monetary history and theory and for his demonstration of the complexity of stabilization policy" [E52]
- 1981 James Tobin: "for his analysis of financial markets and their relations to expenditure decisions, employment, production and prices" [E44]
- 1984 Richard Stone: "for having made fundamental contributions to the development of systems of national accounts and hence greatly improved the basis for empirical economic analysis" [E01]
- 1985 Franco Modigliani: "for his pioneering analyses of saving and of financial markets" [E21]
- 1995 Robert E. Lucas Jr.: "for having developed and applied the hypothesis of rational expectations, and thereby having transformed macroeconomic analysis and deepened our understanding of economic policy" [E13]
- 2004 Finn E. Kydland; Edward C. Prescott: "for their contributions to dynamic macroeconomics: the time consistency of economic policy and the driving forces behind business cycles" [E32]
- 2006 Edmund S. Phelps: "for his analysis of intertemporal tradeoffs in macroeconomic policy" [E61]

Trade (3 prize works):
- 1977 Bertil Ohlin; James E. Meade: "for their pathbreaking contribution to the theory of international trade and international capital movements" [F11]
- 1999 Robert Mundell: "for his analysis of monetary and fiscal policy under different exchange rate regimes and his analysis of optimum currency areas" [F41]
- 2008 Paul Krugman: "for his analysis of trade patterns and location of economic activity" [F12]

Production, Industrial Organization (5 prize works):
- 1978 Herbert Simon: "for his pioneering research into the decision-making process within economic organizations" [D23]
- 1982 George J. Stigler: "for his seminal studies of industrial structures, functioning of markets and causes and effects of public regulation" [L51]
- 1991 Ronald H. Coase: "for his discovery and clarification of the significance of transaction costs and property rights for the institutional structure and functioning of the economy" [D23]
- 2009 Oliver E. Williamson: "for his analysis of economic governance, especially the boundaries of the firm" [D23]
- 2014 Jean Tirole: "for his analysis of market power and regulation" [L51]

Public, Law, Political Economy (2 prize works):
- 1986 James M. Buchanan Jr.: "for his development of the contractual and constitutional bases for the theory of economic and political decision-making" [D72]
- 2009 Elinor Ostrom: "for her analysis of economic governance, especially the commons" [D02]

Equilibrium, Welfare (7 prize works):
- 1970 Paul A. Samuelson: "for the scientific work through which he has developed static and dynamic economic theory and actively contributed to raising the level of analysis in economic science" [C61]
- 1972 John R. Hicks; Kenneth J. Arrow: "for their pioneering contributions to general economic equilibrium theory and welfare theory" [D51]
- 1973 Wassily Leontief: "for the development of the input-output method and for its application to important economic problems" [C67]
- 1975 Leonid Vitaliyevich Kantorovich; Tjalling C. Koopmans: "for their contributions to the theory of optimum allocation of resources" [C61]
- 1983 Gerard Debreu: "for having incorporated new analytical methods into economic theory and for his rigorous reformulation of the theory of general equilibrium" [D51]
- 1988 Maurice Allais: "for his pioneering contributions to the theory of markets and efficient utilization of resources" [D51]
- 1998 Amartya Sen: "for his contributions to welfare economics" [D63]
