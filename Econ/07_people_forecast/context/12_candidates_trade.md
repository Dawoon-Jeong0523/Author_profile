# Candidates for the 2026 Prize in Economic Sciences: Trade

Field: Trade (JEL F): international trade, international finance and open-economy macroeconomics, exchange rates. Field forecast: 22.5 % of the five fields of the pool (note 01).

The 7 candidates below are combinations of one to three people with the contribution a prize would recognize. They were nominated by a simulated committee (eleven personas reconstructed from the published research records of the members of the 2026 prize committee, each asked on three language models without web access) and integrated across the models; the defining works are the publications the nominations cite as establishing the contribution. The list is a candidate set, not a statement of the real committee members' views. Previous laureates and members of the 2026 committee are not included; the living status of the people was screened on Wikidata, and people named by the committee who have died are noted and left out of the lineups.

1. Marc J. Melitz (b. 1968), Andrew B. Bernard (b. 1963), Elhanan Helpman (b. 1946)
   Contribution: for the analysis of firm heterogeneity, selection and reallocation in international trade [F12]
   Defining works:
   - Melitz, 2003: The Impact of Trade on Intra-Industry Reallocations and Aggregate Industry Productivity, Econometrica
   - Bernard and Jensen, 1995: Exporters, Jobs, and Wages in U.S. Manufacturing: 1976-1987, Brookings Papers on Economic Activity: Microeconomics
   - Helpman, Melitz and Yeaple, 2004: Export versus FDI with Heterogeneous Firms, American Economic Review
   - Bernard and Jensen, 1999: Exceptional Exporter Performance: Cause, Effect, or Both?, Journal of International Economics

2. Samuel Kortum (b. 1960)
   Contribution: for developing quantitative Ricardian general-equilibrium models of trade, technology and geography [F11]
   Note: Jonathan Eaton, named by the committee for this contribution, died in 2024 and is not eligible.
   Defining works:
   - Eaton and Kortum, 2002: Technology, Geography, and Trade, Econometrica
   - Eaton, Kortum and Kramarz, 2011: An Anatomy of International Trade, Econometrica
   - Eaton and Kortum, 1999: International Technology Diffusion: Theory and Measurement, International Economic Review

3. Gene M. Grossman (b. 1955), Elhanan Helpman (b. 1946)
   Contribution: for the analysis of innovation, growth and the political economy of trade policy [F13]
   Defining works:
   - Grossman and Helpman, 1994: Protection for Sale, American Economic Review
   - Grossman and Helpman, 1991: Innovation and Growth in the Global Economy, MIT Press
   - Grossman and Helpman, 2001: Special Interest Politics, MIT Press
   - Grossman and Helpman, 1995: Trade Wars and Trade Talks, Journal of Political Economy

4. Maurice Obstfeld (b. 1952), Kenneth Rogoff (b. 1953)
   Contribution: for founding the micro-founded intertemporal new open-economy macroeconomics [F41]
   Defining works:
   - Obstfeld and Rogoff, 1995: Exchange Rate Dynamics Redux, Journal of Political Economy
   - Obstfeld and Rogoff, 1996: Foundations of International Macroeconomics, MIT Press
   - Obstfeld and Rogoff, 2000: The Six Major Puzzles in International Macroeconomics: Is There a Common Cause?, NBER Macroeconomics Annual

5. David H. Autor (b. 1967), David Dorn (b. 1979), Gordon H. Hanson (b. 1964)
   Contribution: for the empirical analysis of the local labor-market effects of import competition [F16]
   Defining works:
   - Autor, Dorn and Hanson, 2013: The China Syndrome: Local Labor Market Effects of Import Competition in the United States, American Economic Review
   - Autor, Dorn, Hanson and Song, 2014: Trade Adjustment: Worker-Level Evidence, Quarterly Journal of Economics
   - Autor, Dorn and Hanson, 2016: The China Shock: Learning from Labor-Market Adjustment to Large Changes in Trade, Annual Review of Economics

6. Pol Antràs (b. 1975), Gene M. Grossman (b. 1955), Esteban Rossi-Hansberg (b. 1973)
   Contribution: for the analysis of offshoring, incomplete contracts and the organization of global value chains [F23]
   Defining works:
   - Antràs, 2003: Firms, Contracts, and Trade Structure, Quarterly Journal of Economics
   - Grossman and Rossi-Hansberg, 2008: Trading Tasks: A Simple Theory of Offshoring, American Economic Review
   - Antràs and Helpman, 2004: Global Sourcing, Journal of Political Economy
   - Grossman and Helpman, 2002: Integration versus Outsourcing in Industry Equilibrium, Quarterly Journal of Economics

7. James E. Anderson (b. 1940), Eric van Wincoop (b. 1963)
   Contribution: for the theoretical foundation of structural gravity and the measurement of trade costs [F14]
   Defining works:
   - Anderson, 1979: A Theoretical Foundation for the Gravity Equation, American Economic Review
   - Anderson and van Wincoop, 2003: Gravity with Gravitas: A Solution to the Border Puzzle, American Economic Review
   - Anderson and van Wincoop, 2004: Trade Costs, Journal of Economic Literature
