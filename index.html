Nobel 2026 · Preseen context experiment

Do bibliometric profile cards of the candidates change a forecasting system's probabilities for the 2026 Nobel Prizes in Physiology or Medicine, Physics and Chemistry? Forecasts were made before the announcements (5, 6 and 7 October 2026).

Dashboard 1 · 1 October 2026

Cards as context to consider

Control runs without context against runs with the profile cards attached (treatment “consider”): effects against run-to-run noise, per field and pooled, with the forecasters' write-ups.

Told to rely on the cards

Two follow-up arms with an instruction note: cards as the main evidence, and cards as one of the main sources alongside prizes, news and predictions; where each forecast lands against the control and cross-check runs.
